Mi-CAT

Mi-CAT is an ML-integrated catastrophe modelling stack with three integrated layers: (1) AI learns asset-level vulnerability patterns from observed damage data, capturing association factors that archetype-based functions ignore; (2) AI fuses fragmented multi-source data into harmonized hazard, exposure and loss bases at the asset and sub-national level; (3) outputs are coupled with macro-fiscal models for budget planning and prevention prioritization.

We use machine learning to learn asset-level vulnerability functions from observed damage data (Random Forests, gradient boosting, generalized additive models) and to fuse multi-source data — Earth observation via Google Earth Engine, infrastructure layers, governmental loss databases — through Gaussian processes and spatial interpolation. Outputs will be coupled with macro-fiscal stress-testing models. Stack: Python (scikit-learn, XGBoost, GeoPandas), R, PostGIS, Oasis LMF, and MCP-enabled cloud-native pipelines for reproducible scaling.
